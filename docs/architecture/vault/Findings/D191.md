# Workflow scheduling claims and attempt authority are different snapshots

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

execute checks admitted hashes through dry_run, then caches authority_claims once. _run_node resolves current binding/executor/grants again and signs those hashes without comparing them to original admission or cached scope claims. Worker validates fresh hashes. A later admitted authority change can therefore alter scope/adapter assumptions after scheduling; this interleaving was not reproduced.
