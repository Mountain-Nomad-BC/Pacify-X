# Archive thresholds flag metadata after its allocation rather than bound parsing

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

ZipFile loads infolist before max_entries and aggregate thresholds are checked. Optional maps and hazard sets can grow with all entries; no bound limits the archive byte hashing or central-directory parse. Floating NaN thresholds satisfy the simple positivity check but disable greater-than ratio comparisons.
