# Maturity level semantics depend on the supplied policy

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

Installed policy requires installed-host evidence at L5 and external-authority at L6. General policy validator checks ordered levels, unique nonempty evidence types and nondecreasing rank, so a custom policy may weaken those requirements. Architecture helper adds an L5/L6 stronger-authority predicate, but evaluator does not invoke that helper. verified means at least L0, not all operational levels.
