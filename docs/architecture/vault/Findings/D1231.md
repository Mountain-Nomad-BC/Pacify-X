# Benchmark memory and rewrite metrics have narrower meaning than their labels

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

Without psutil RSS is zero and budget passes. RSS sampling covers cold decode only; no process-isolated baseline. projection_rewrite_bytes_per_append compares snapshot endpoints, excludes head and cumulative delta writes, and is not divided by append count. All samples are few warm observations, not tail-latency population guarantees.
