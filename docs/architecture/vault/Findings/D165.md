# WAL recovery consistency is not concurrent reader isolation

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

Targets are replaced sequentially under a per-journal writer lock. Crash tests recover then assert the complete before/after set. A reader outside that lock can see a mixed generation while applying; another journal does not share the writer lock. The durable guarantee depends on retained manifest recovery, not one filesystem operation for all targets.
