# Authority transaction is compensated multi-file publication

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

register_authority_transaction prevalidates records and locks writers, but _resolve readers do not take that lock. Records and history are published sequentially; exception rollback is another sequence of writes and can fail. Initial project/key creation also has no shared initialization lock. Authentication does not imply atomic authority-set visibility.
