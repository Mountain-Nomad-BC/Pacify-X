# Section chunk scheduling preserves all pending results rather than failing fast

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

All pending chunks are submitted under max_parallel_chunks and individual timeouts. Worker Exceptions become failing chunk receipts while siblings continue. Aggregate duration measures scheduling time; no separate aggregate deadline or cancellation-on-first-failure exists here. BaseException and failure while publishing the failure receipt escape this per-worker conversion.
