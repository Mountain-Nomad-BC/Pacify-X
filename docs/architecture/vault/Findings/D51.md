# Recovery health is bounded by configured authorities

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

RecoveryCoordinator runs configured WAL/state/callback checks. Missing event/session/resource callback groups may be healthy with configured=False. Doctor passes only its known existing WAL roots and apply=False, and downgrades an absence of discovered WALs to degraded. A healthy reconciliation is not a claim that every possible state owner was examined.
