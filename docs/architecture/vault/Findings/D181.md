# Freshness policies differ across evidence consumers

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

TrustedEvidenceResolver rejects records older than max_age but not future timestamps; its now is captured at construction. Effect grants require expiry after issue and current before expiry, without current at or after issue. Pure evidence assembler explicitly rejects future evidence. These are three distinct temporal contracts.
