# Lifecycle preparation retention differs from registered draft workspace closure

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

Draft staging registers its temporary workspace and preserves committed-create truth through cleanup warnings. Lifecycle preparation creates .preparing and canonical .lifecycle directories directly; recovery retains .preparing entries and does not register/reclaim these through ResourceManager. _atomic_bytes write-loop failure precedes its prepared-file cleanup block. Retention can therefore include incomplete preparation debris distinct from durable signed journal custody.
