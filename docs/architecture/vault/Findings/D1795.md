# Knowledge and coordination waiters are not uniformly request-bound

**Historical finding; current closure not revalidated.**

Knowledge operations/browse and coordination results often match type/kind/operation after an offset. Browse may precede the mutation and another same-operation result may match. Selected proposal/head/hash and task/confirmed checks are useful but do not establish all request/state ordering or full durable content.
