## Engineering baseline

- [ ] Intended and actual changes match.
- [ ] Relevant tests and static checks pass.
- [ ] Security, data, dependency, accessibility, and deployment effects are reviewed.
- [ ] Rollback remains viable.
- [ ] Evidence is current, scoped, and sanitized.
- [ ] Independent outcome verification passes.

## Completion rule

A successful command, tool call, build, or executor claim is not sufficient evidence of completion.
