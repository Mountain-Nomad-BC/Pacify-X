# Research candidate contract

Required fields:

- source citation, version/date, license, and source hash;
- claim and whether it is directly supported, inferred, or unsupported;
- method, assumptions, prerequisites, dataset/environment, and limitations;
- operational value and mapped capability gap;
- bounded I/O, effects, dependencies, cost, latency, risk, and rollback;
- candidate skill/orchestration/adapter type;
- reproduction, negative, effect-boundary, and integration tests;
- local validation evidence and admission status.

Publication, benchmark, or anecdote is evidence about a setting—not production proof for another setting.
