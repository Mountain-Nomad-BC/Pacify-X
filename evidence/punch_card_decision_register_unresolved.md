## Classification policy reminders
- `exact_typed_controls` is used when implementation is bound to identifiable executable/UI controls.
- `non_visible_path` is used only when no user-facing control exists and requirement is implemented via runtime/test/host path.
- `aggregate_split` is used where a card is decomposed into traceable split control/work units that must close through children.
- `aggregate_parent` applies only when child cards are established and carry authoritative ownership.
- `governed_retirement` is reserved for obsolete/superseded/duplicate cards after authority review.
