# Sources and standards

Verify material current facts at commissioning time. Prefer official legislation and regulator publications for legal obligations; official standards bodies for accessibility, security, privacy, identity, and interoperability; vendor documentation for platform capabilities, versions, limits, support, and pricing; package registries and signed provenance for dependencies; and project-owned runtime evidence for existing-system claims.

Record title, publisher, stable URL or identifier, publication/version date, access date, jurisdiction or applicability, and the exact decision supported. A citation is evidence for a claim, not proof that a proposed implementation satisfies it.
